Silver Sage SLC-1, first note of the season. Jenn F. 3/5/26

For anyone new reading this file: the four towers here (CT-A through D) run
the HIGH CYCLES program, CT-HC2. They are supposed to sit way above the
normal conductivity limit. Do not panic at 2900 and do not compare these to
the standard tower table. The tradeoff is we watch chloride and keep the
CT-785 residual up. That is the deal with the customer to save water.

All four towers in range today. CT-B chloride a touch higher than the
others, worth keeping an eye on.

BMS trend export is set up to come over monthly. Handy but remember it is
only conductivity and sometimes pH, it is not a substitute for wet tests.
