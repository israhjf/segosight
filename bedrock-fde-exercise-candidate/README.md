# Bedrock AI: Forward-Deployed Engineering Exercise

Start with `candidate-brief.pdf`. It contains the full assignment,
constraints, deliverables, and evaluation criteria. The same content is in
`candidate-brief.md` if you prefer plain text.

`materials/` contains everything the client could give you: two reference
PDFs, six CSV extracts, technician field notes, a service inbox export, and
a second batch of September data in `materials/new_data_batch/`.

Practical notes:

- Everything here is fictional and synthetic. All companies, people,
  addresses, emails, and phone numbers are invented for this exercise.
- The data is intentionally imperfect. That is the terrain, not a bug in
  the packet. There is no cleaner copy to request.
- You have four calendar days and should spend 12 to 15 hours. You are not
  expected to spend any money on tools, hosting, or model access.
- Questions go to your Bedrock contact. Silence on a detail is part of the
  exercise: document your assumption and keep moving.
