SITE NOTE - Bonneville Foods, Ogden Plant
Marcus Webb, 7/28/26

Unit 2 boiler (SYS-0006) again. Iron came in at 1.3 ppm today. Every single
reading is still under the 2.0 action limit in the book, but I have watched
this number walk up from 0.25 in March, month after month, same direction
every time. A boiler does not do that for no reason. The book's own trend
language says a sustained rise like this is active corrosion and should be
investigated, whatever the single-sample limit says.

Found rust staining at the DA vent and the condensate receiver overflow.
Amine feed keeps getting shut off by their operators when the smell bothers
them at the packaging line, so condensate pH sits on the low side and we
eat metal a little at a time.

Told Teresa's day supervisor. Recommending a proper condensate survey and
an internal inspection at the fall outage. Putting it in my notes because I
have said it out loud twice now and it has gone nowhere. This is a 2005
boiler carrying their whole process line.
